# Shiftat

**Connecting businesses with flexible talent.**

## Overview

Shiftat is a temporary employment platform designed to connect businesses and individuals seeking short-term work opportunities.

The platform simplifies temporary recruitment by helping businesses find suitable workers based on their skills, experience, location, and availability, while giving job seekers access to flexible employment opportunities.

Shiftat aims to make temporary hiring more accessible, efficient, and reliable within the Egyptian job market.

## Problem Statement

Finding temporary workers can be time-consuming and unreliable for businesses, while individuals seeking short-term employment often struggle to find relevant opportunities.

Traditional recruitment platforms primarily focus on permanent employment, leaving a gap for people looking for flexible, short-term work.

Shiftat addresses this gap through a centralized platform that facilitates temporary job discovery, candidate matching, and recruitment.

## Objectives

- Simplify the process of finding and hiring temporary workers.
- Connect job seekers with relevant short-term opportunities.
- Improve recruitment through location-based and profile-based matching.
- Reduce the time and effort required for temporary recruitment.
- Provide a more reliable environment through profile verification, ratings, and reviews.

## Target Users

### 1. Job Seekers
- University students.
- Individuals seeking additional income.
- Short-term and temporary job seekers.
- People looking for flexible working hours.

### 2. Businesses
- Small and medium-sized businesses.
- Companies requiring temporary staff.
- Event organizers.
- Businesses with seasonal or occasional staffing needs.

## Core Workflow

1. **Registration:** Job seekers and business owners create accounts on the platform.
2. **Profile Setup:** Job seekers provide their skills, experience, location, and availability.
3. **Job Posting:** Businesses publish temporary job opportunities with their requirements, location, duration, and compensation.
4. **Candidate Matching:** The system identifies and recommends suitable candidates based on their profiles and job requirements.
5. **Candidate Selection:** Businesses review recommended candidates and send invitations to suitable job seekers.
6. **Invitation Response:** Job seekers can accept or decline invitations.
7. **Job Confirmation:** The business confirms the selected worker and the job status is updated.
8. **Feedback:** Both parties can provide ratings and reviews after the job is completed.

## Key Features

- User registration and authentication.
- Separate job seeker and business profiles.
- Temporary job posting and management.
- Skills-based candidate matching.
- Location-based job matching.
- Availability-based recommendations.
- Candidate invitations and response tracking.
- Job status management.
- Profile verification.
- Ratings and reviews.
- Notifications.
- Worker backup recommendations.

## Project Scope

Shiftat focuses on the temporary employment recruitment process, from account registration and job posting to candidate selection, invitation management, and post-job feedback.

The system is designed to facilitate connections between businesses and job seekers rather than operate as a traditional permanent recruitment platform.
